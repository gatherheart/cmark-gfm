# Tolerant emphasis

Cases collected from author expectation, parsed with
CMARK_OPT_TOLERANT_EMPHASIS. Case numbers match the design spec at
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-04-cmark-gfm-tolerant-emphasis-design.md

Cases 4 and 11 are absent by design: case 4 (intraword _) is out of scope to
protect snake_case identifiers, and case 11 duplicates case 2.

## Case 1 - loose closing delimiter (R1", R1')

```````````````````````````````` example
**안녕하세요 **
.
<p><strong>안녕하세요 </strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Case 2 - loose opening delimiter (R1", R1')

```````````````````````````````` example
** 안녕하세요**
.
<p><strong> 안녕하세요</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Case 3 - loose closer after punctuation (R1", R1')

```````````````````````````````` example
**안녕하세요! **
.
<p><strong>안녕하세요! </strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Case 5 - crossing bold and italic (R3, R4, R6, R8, R9)

```````````````````````````````` example
**12*34**56*
.
<p><strong>12<em>34</em></strong><em>56</em></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Case 6 - crossing strikethrough and bold (R4, R8, R9)

```````````````````````````````` example strikethrough
~~가나**다라~~마바**
.
<p><del>가나<strong>다라</strong></del><strong>마바</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Case 7 - two loose pairs on one line, case 1 plus case 2 (R1", R1')

```````````````````````````````` example
**안녕 **하세요** 반가워**
.
<p><strong>안녕 </strong>하세요<strong> 반가워</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Case 8 - bold and italic together (already correct)

```````````````````````````````` example
***안녕하세요!***
.
<p><em><strong>안녕하세요!</strong></em></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Case 9 - literal asterisk inside italic (already correct)

```````````````````````````````` example
*가격은 * 입니다*
.
<p><em>가격은 * 입니다</em></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Case 10 - truncated closing delimiter (R4, R6)

```````````````````````````````` example
**안녕하세요*
.
<p><strong>안녕하세요</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Case 12 - lone interior asterisk stays literal (R4, R6)

```````````````````````````````` example
**굵게*기울임**
.
<p><strong>굵게*기울임</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Regression - adjacent ranges stay separate (R4)

```````````````````````````````` example
**a** and **b**
.
<p><strong>a</strong> and <strong>b</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Regression - snake_case untouched (case 4 out of scope)

```````````````````````````````` example
snake_case_name
.
<p>snake_case_name</p>
````````````````````````````````

## Regression - dunder untouched (pins current behavior)

```````````````````````````````` example
__init__
.
<p><strong>init</strong></p>
````````````````````````````````

## Regression - screaming snake untouched

```````````````````````````````` example
MAX_BUFFER_SIZE
.
<p>MAX_BUFFER_SIZE</p>
````````````````````````````````

## Regression - single asterisk arithmetic (R1" length gate)

```````````````````````````````` example
2 * 3 * 4 = 24
.
<p>2 * 3 * 4 = 24</p>
````````````````````````````````

## Regression - single asterisk prose (R1" length gate)

```````````````````````````````` example
a * b, c * d
.
<p>a * b, c * d</p>
````````````````````````````````

## Regression - both edges loose is rejected (R1')

```````````````````````````````` example
2 ** 3 ** 4
.
<p>2 ** 3 ** 4</p>
````````````````````````````````
